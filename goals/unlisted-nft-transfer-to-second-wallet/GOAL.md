# Unlisted NFT transfer to second wallet

Goal ID: goal_32c4aa0ee602
Goal slug: unlisted-nft-transfer-to-second-wallet

## Description
Send the main wallet's unlisted NFTs to the user's second wallet, excluding listed, soulbound, and user-excluded tokens. Ownership of 21 main-wallet NFTs was verified, with Runestone, Mintra, and Ptah excluded after the user's update, along with BinanceMiner and the already-listed SAFU and CIOW tokens.
