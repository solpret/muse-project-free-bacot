        <h1>Xigo (XIGO) — hourly US-stock dividends on BNB Smart Chain</h1>
        <p>Xigo (XIGO) is a token on BNB Smart Chain (chain ID 56). There are <strong>3,333 shares</strong> of <strong>4,000,000 XIGO</strong> each. The <strong>first 1,000 shares are free</strong> (one per wallet, gas only); the other <strong>2,333 cost BNB worth 1 USDT each</strong> (live price from the PancakeSwap BNB/USDT 30-minute TWAP; overpayment refunded). Only wallets holding a <strong>Binance Agent ID</strong> can take part; paid tier max 12 shares per wallet; mint window 72 hours; the pool launches automatically when the paid tier sells out (or anyone can launch with the paid shares sold after the window ends). Total supply is capped at 20,797,600,000 XIGO.</p>
        <p>每小时分红美股代币。共 3,333 份，每份 4,000,000 细狗：前 1,000 份免费领取（每个钱包 1 份，只付 gas），其余 2,333 份每份价值 1 USDT 的 BNB。仅限持有币安 Agent ID 的钱包，铸造期 72 小时，付费档售罄自动开池。</p>

        <h2>Where the money goes</h2>
        <ul>
          <li>Paid BNB: 80% is swapped to USDT and paired with 3,200,000 XIGO per paid share in the XIGO/USDT liquidity pool. LP tokens are locked in the LiquidityVault contract, which has no withdraw function. 20% goes to the treasury wallet <code>0xb909C1228145BE84cFDf7A7d02612B70F4b543Da</code>. Free shares bring in no BNB.</li>
          <li>Trading fee 3% on buys and sells: 60% to the pool, 20% to hourly stock dividends, 20% to the treasury wallet. The fee can only decrease, never increase.</li>
          <li>Dividends: every hour the dividend share is swapped through real PancakeSwap V3 pools (30-minute TWAP, ±3% protection) into NVDAB, SPCXB, AAPLB, GOOGLB and MSFTB (tokenized NVIDIA, SpaceX, Apple, Google, Microsoft) and shared pro rata to holders. Anyone can trigger the hourly conversion.</li>
        </ul>

        <h2>How to mint (humans and AI agents)</h2>
        <ol>
          <li>Use a wallet that holds a Binance Agent ID (Binance Agent Wallet or any wallet holding the ID).</li>
          <li>Free share: if <code>freeAvailableFor(address)</code> returns 1, call <code>claimFree()</code> (no BNB, gas only).</li>
          <li>Paid shares: when <code>paidOpen()</code> is true, read <code>quote(uint256 n)</code> for the BNB needed, then call <code>mint(uint256 n)</code> with msg.value ≥ quote (add ~3% buffer; excess is refunded).</li>
          <li>Verify with <code>freeClaimed(address)</code> and <code>minted(address)</code>.</li>
        </ol>
